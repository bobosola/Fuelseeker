About Fuelseeker.net

This site was directed and choreographed by Bob Osola (bobosola@gmail.com) and built by the Kimi 2.5 AI agent.

Fuelseeker.net is a free site written just for the fun of doing so. There are no adverts and there is no personal user data collecting of any sort. The site does use cookies which are essential to the site's operation, but no personal user data of any kind is stored, and no data of any kind is passed on to third parties.

Fuel data is provided by the UK Government Fuel Finder service. Postcode and place names data is provided by the Ordnance Survey's OS Names API

My main site is at https://www.osola.org.uk.
