About Fuelseeker.net

This site was choreographed by me and built by the Kimi 2.5 AI coding agent. The fuel data is updated several times daily.

Fuelseeker.net is a free site written just for the fun of doing so. There are no adverts and no data collecting cookies. I don't want to know your email address, your height, or the colour of your socks. No data of any kind is passed on to anyone (because I don't collect any).

Free fuel data is provided by the UK Government Fuel Finder service. Free postcode and place names data is provided by the Ordnance Survey's OS Names API

My main site is at https://www.osola.org.uk. You can contact me at bobosola@gmail.com to report any problems or if you have any ideas for improvements.
