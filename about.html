About Fuelseeker.net

This site was directed and choreographed by me, Bob Osola and built by the Kimi 2.5 AI coding agent. The fuel data is updated daily.

Fuelseeker.net is a free site written just for the fun of doing so. There are no adverts and no personal user data collecting of any sort. I don't want to know your email address, your height, or the colour of your socks. The site does use temporary cookies for technical reasons to enable the search features, but no personal user data of any kind is stored, and no data of any kind is passed on to third parties (because I don't collect any). The cookies are deleted automatically when you close the browser tab.

Free fuel data is provided by the UK Government Fuel Finder service. Free Postcode and place names data is provided by the Ordnance Survey's OS Names API

My main site is at https://www.osola.org.uk. You can contact me at bobosola@gmail.com to report any problems or if you have any great ideas for new features.
